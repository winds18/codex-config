# Refero Style Taxonomy

This reference compresses the style-selection patterns behind Refero-style design prompts into a reusable taxonomy.

Use it when the user says things like:

- "做得高级一点"
- "想要 SaaS 感"
- "要更像开发者产品"
- "希望有 AI 创业产品的感觉"
- "想让页面更有质感，但不要营销味太重"

## Quick Choice

- Choose `clean-saas` for clarity, trust, conversion, and mainstream software buyers.
- Choose `dark-mode` for premium contrast, product glow, and high-focus technical surfaces.
- Choose `devtool` for developer trust, technical proof, and workflow credibility.
- Choose `editorial` for thoughtful storytelling, brand intelligence, and calm product framing.
- Choose `ai-startup` for frontier energy, fast-moving novelty, and visible "future product" signaling.
- Choose `dashboard-workbench` for dense operational interfaces, internal tools, and scanning-heavy workflows.

If torn between two styles, recommend one primary direction and keep one backup.

## Clean SaaS

**Best for**

- landing pages
- pricing pages
- feature pages
- onboarding
- product marketing for B2B software

**Signals**

- clean hierarchy
- bright or warm-light surfaces
- obvious product promise
- restrained component styling
- trustworthy conversion flow

**Show**

- hero claim tied to product result
- feature proof
- screenshots
- pricing anchors
- customer trust marks

**Avoid**

- excessive gradients
- decorative 3D clutter
- dense technical jargon unless the audience expects it

**Prompt fragments**

- "Use a calm, polished SaaS visual system with clear hierarchy and visible product proof."
- "The page should feel trustworthy, legible, and conversion-aware rather than experimental."

## Dark Mode

**Best for**

- premium product pages
- developer tools
- AI products
- command-center dashboards
- cinematic launch surfaces

**Signals**

- dark backgrounds with readable contrast
- controlled accent color
- elevated surfaces
- luminous product highlights
- premium focus

**Show**

- product screenshots
- terminal or editor surfaces
- strong contrast states
- restrained highlight color

**Avoid**

- low-contrast body text
- neon overload
- muddy surfaces without clear elevation

**Prompt fragments**

- "Define a complete dark surface system: background, elevated panels, borders, muted text, accent color, focus states, and hover states."
- "Keep the palette restrained so the UI feels premium, not noisy."

## Devtool

**Best for**

- infrastructure tools
- engineering platforms
- API products
- docs-home hybrids
- technical product marketing

**Signals**

- obvious workflow context
- code, docs, logs, or benchmark proof
- denser information layout
- precise UI language
- real product evidence

**Show**

- code snippets
- integrations
- docs links
- benchmark or reliability proof
- product screenshots
- workflow diagrams

**Avoid**

- fluffy claims without evidence
- oversized marketing hero layouts
- empty decorative illustrations standing in for proof

**Prompt fragments**

- "Show the technical job this product performs and where it sits in the developer workflow."
- "Use dense but readable hierarchy with product proof developers recognize."

## Editorial

**Best for**

- founder-led brands
- product essays
- thoughtful software brands
- portfolio-like product storytelling
- research-heavy positioning

**Signals**

- quiet typography
- careful spacing
- warm or paper-like surfaces
- thoughtful pacing
- fewer but stronger visual moments

**Show**

- narrative sections
- selective product screenshots
- diagrams
- quotes
- concept framing

**Avoid**

- loud badge spam
- overly game-like gradients
- card soup with no narrative rhythm

**Prompt fragments**

- "Use an editorial rhythm with calm typography, intelligent spacing, and a more thoughtful pacing than a standard SaaS page."
- "The page should feel authored and considered, not templated."

## AI Startup

**Best for**

- new AI products
- frontier brand launches
- research-to-product announcements
- products that need novelty and energy

**Signals**

- future-facing tone
- visual energy
- strong product thesis
- bold but controlled accenting
- fast-moving category cues

**Show**

- product workflow
- before/after value
- model or agent interaction
- use-case coverage
- launch credibility

**Avoid**

- generic cosmic art with no product meaning
- hype without workflow clarity
- pure vibes replacing interface detail

**Prompt fragments**

- "Give the page frontier energy, but keep the product workflow legible."
- "Make the novelty visible through the UI and use cases, not just through gradients."

## Dashboard Workbench

**Best for**

- internal tools
- ops dashboards
- analyst tools
- admin panels
- workflow software with repeated use

**Signals**

- dense but stable layout
- predictable controls
- restrained surfaces
- strong table and filter ergonomics
- scanning over spectacle

**Show**

- information hierarchy
- filters
- tables
- charts
- detail panels
- real workflow actions

**Avoid**

- giant hero sections
- decorative cards for whole-page sections
- soft marketing composition that hides the work

**Prompt fragments**

- "Design this like a high-usage workbench: dense, organized, and easy to scan repeatedly."
- "Favor strong information hierarchy and tool ergonomics over decorative storytelling."

## Selection Heuristics

Use these tie-breakers:

- If trust and conversion matter most, lean `clean-saas`.
- If technical credibility matters most, lean `devtool`.
- If density and repeated workflow matter most, lean `dashboard-workbench`.
- If premium contrast and visual drama matter most, lean `dark-mode`.
- If thoughtfulness and authored pacing matter most, lean `editorial`.
- If frontier energy matters most, lean `ai-startup`.
